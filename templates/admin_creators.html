💡 How Creators Work

  • Create a creator profile — give them a name, avatar, cover, and flag
  • Creators and managers upload from the Creator Dashboard; admins can override via Admin → Profiles → Upload
  • Use Edit Profile to add posts that appear on their public page
  • Viewers can like, comment, and reply on each creator's posts
  • Revenue is tracked per creator — view it in Analytics
