💡 How Categories Work

  • Categories are browsable at /categories on the public site
  • Creators pick a category in the Creator Dashboard upload form — names must match an active category here exactly
  • The order number controls sorting (lower = appears first)
  • Deleting a category does not delete content — items just appear uncategorized
  • You can set categories to Photos only, Videos only, or Both
